# UGC.AI

UGC.AI helps creators and brands turn a product photo and a model photo into promotional content for social media.

## What the app does

1. Sign in and upload a product image and a model image.
2. Add the product details, choose a portrait or landscape format, and optionally describe the result you want.
3. Generate an image that places the model and product together in a promotional scene.
4. Turn the generated image into a short product video.
5. Download, share, publish, or delete saved generations from your account.
6. Browse content that other users have published to the community.
7. Open the public demo workspace to explore the product with sample content and no account setup.

Image and video generation uses credits. If a generation fails, the credits used for that request are returned to the account.
